Performance Calculation Notes
• TCH: Total cane yield divided by area.
• Weighted ERS: Total tons sugar divided by total cane yield, multiplied by 100.
• TSH: Total tons sugar divided by area.
• Average Bundle Weight: Cane yield divided by bundles.
• Grower Totals: Consolidate all displayed field records belonging to the same grower.
• Seedcane ERS: Uses the established 14.10% value.
• Consolidated Reports: Combines records from all saved reports for the selected season when All Reports is selected.
